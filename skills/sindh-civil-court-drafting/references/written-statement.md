# Written Statement (Order VIII CPC)

A written statement is the defendant's formal reply to the plaint. It has four blocks in this order.

## Skeleton

```
IN THE COURT OF THE [designation] CIVIL JUDGE, [city], SINDH

[F.C.] Suit No. ______ of 20__

[Full name], S/o [ ], R/o [ ]                         ...... Plaintiff

              VERSUS

1. [Full name], S/o [ ], R/o [ ]                      ...... Defendant No. 1
2. ...
6. Heirs of [ ] (deceased), namely [ ]                ...... Defendant No. 6
                                            (and others as arrayed)

WRITTEN STATEMENT ON BEHALF OF DEFENDANT NO. [ ]

Respectfully Sheweth:

PRELIMINARY LEGAL OBJECTIONS

1. That the suit is not maintainable in its present form and is liable to be dismissed.
2. That the plaintiff has no cause of action against this defendant.
3. That the suit is barred by limitation / by law [specify].
4. That the plaint is bad for mis-joinder / non-joinder of necessary parties.
5. That the suit is undervalued for the purposes of court fee and jurisdiction.
6. That the suit is barred by the principle of res judicata / estoppel [if applicable].
   [Add only the objections that actually apply to the facts.]

REPLY ON FACTS (PARAWISE)

1. That the contents of para 1 of the plaint are [admitted / denied / not admitted for want of knowledge]. [State this defendant's version.]
2. That the contents of para 2 of the plaint are denied. It is denied that [ ]. The correct position is that [ ].
   [Continue with one numbered reply for EACH numbered paragraph of the plaint.]

ADDITIONAL PLEAS

[ ]. That [set out the defendant's affirmative case / new facts not in the plaint].

PRAYER

It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Honourable Court may be pleased to
dismiss the suit of the plaintiff with costs, and grant any other relief this Honourable
Court deems fit in the circumstances.

                                                    Defendant No. [ ]
                                        Through: [Advocate], Advocate

VERIFICATION
Verified at [city] on this ___ day of ______ 20__ that the contents of paras __ to __
are true to my knowledge and paras __ to __ are believed to be true on legal advice /
information received, and nothing material has been concealed.

                                                    Defendant No. [ ]
```

## Rules specific to a written statement

- **One reply per plaint paragraph.** Match the plaint's numbering exactly. If the plaint has 22 paras, the reply-on-facts block has 22 numbered paragraphs.
- **Deny specifically.** Under Order VIII Rr. 3–5 CPC an evasive or general denial ("the plaintiff is put to strict proof") is treated as an admission for allegations not specifically dealt with. For each material allegation, state clearly whether it is admitted, denied, or not admitted, and give the defendant's version.
- **Preliminary objections are legal, not factual.** Keep maintainability, limitation, jurisdiction, valuation, mis-joinder here — not mixed into the reply on facts.
- **Additional pleas** carry the defendant's affirmative case (e.g. that a gift/hiba entry is fabricated) that isn't merely a denial of a plaint paragraph.
- **Aligned defendant.** Where this defendant supports the plaintiff's position against a co-defendant, say so in the additional pleas and keep the parawise replies consistent with that stance.
- Keep the verification split between paras true to knowledge and paras on information/belief.
