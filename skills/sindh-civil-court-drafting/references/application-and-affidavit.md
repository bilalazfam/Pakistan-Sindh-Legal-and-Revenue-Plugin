# Interlocutory Applications, Replies & Affidavits

Covers applications filed within a pending suit and the affidavit that supports them, plus replies/counter-affidavits.

## Common application types and their provision

| Application | Provision |
|---|---|
| Temporary injunction | Order 39 Rules 1 & 2 CPC |
| Rejection of plaint | Order 7 Rule 11 CPC |
| Amendment of pleadings | Order 6 Rule 17 CPC |
| Production of documents | Order 13 / relevant rule |
| Attachment before judgment | Order 38 CPC |
| Interim relief / inherent powers | Section 151 CPC |

Always state the correct provision in the title. Confirm the provision with the user if unsure — do not mislabel.

## Application skeleton

```
IN THE COURT OF THE [designation] CIVIL JUDGE, [city], SINDH

[F.C.] Suit No. ______ of 20__

[Plaintiff]  VERSUS  [Defendants]

APPLICATION UNDER [ORDER __ RULE __ / SECTION __] CPC
FOR [purpose, e.g. GRANT OF TEMPORARY INJUNCTION]
FILED ON BEHALF OF [applicant]

Respectfully Sheweth:

1. That [brief recital of the suit and the applicant's status].
2. That [ground 1 — the fact justifying the relief].
3. That the applicant has a prima facie case, the balance of convenience lies in the
   applicant's favour, and irreparable loss will be caused if the relief is refused.
   [the three ingredients for injunction — adapt to the application type]
4. That this application is bona fide and in the interest of justice.

PRAYER

It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Honourable Court may be pleased to
[state exact relief], and pass any other order deemed just and proper.

                                                    Applicant
                                        Through: [Advocate], Advocate
```

## Supporting affidavit skeleton

Every application is supported by an affidavit affirmed before an Oath Commissioner.

```
AFFIDAVIT

I, [full name], S/o [ ], adult, R/o [ ], do hereby state on oath as under:

1. That I am the [applicant / attorney / defendant No. __] in the above matter and am
   fully conversant with the facts of the case.
2. That the accompanying application has been drafted under my instructions and the
   contents thereof are true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief.
3. That whatever is stated above is true and correct; nothing material has been concealed.

                                                    DEPONENT

Verified/affirmed at [city] on ___ / ___ / 20__ that the contents of this affidavit are
true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief.

                                                    DEPONENT
```

## Reply / counter-affidavit

To oppose an application, reply **parawise** to the application (same "one reply per paragraph" rule as a written statement — see written-statement.md), open with any preliminary objections, and support it with an affidavit in the format above. Pray that the application be **dismissed with costs**.

## Rules

- The **prayer must state the exact interim relief** in operative terms the court can grant.
- For an injunction, plead all three ingredients: prima facie case, balance of convenience, irreparable loss.
- The affidavit deponent must be a person competent to swear to the facts (party or duly authorised attorney).
- Do not assert facts in the affidavit that are not in the application.
