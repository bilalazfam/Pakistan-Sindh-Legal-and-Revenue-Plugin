# Plaint (Order VII CPC)

The document that institutes a civil suit. Must contain the particulars required by Order VII Rule 1 CPC.

## Skeleton

```
IN THE COURT OF THE [designation] CIVIL JUDGE, [city], SINDH

[F.C.] Suit No. ______ of 20__

[Full name], S/o [ ], adult, R/o [ ]                  ...... Plaintiff

              VERSUS

1. [Full name], S/o [ ], R/o [ ]                      ...... Defendant No. 1
   [array all defendants]

SUIT FOR [DECLARATION / SPECIFIC PERFORMANCE / RECOVERY OF Rs. __ /
PERMANENT & MANDATORY INJUNCTION / CANCELLATION OF DOCUMENT / RENDITION OF ACCOUNTS]

Respectfully Sheweth:

1. That the plaintiff is [describe status / capacity].
2. That [material facts, one allegation per paragraph, in chronological order].
3. That [ ].
   ...
[ ]. That the cause of action arose on [date] at [place] when [ ], and is continuing.
[ ]. That the subject matter is situated within the territorial jurisdiction of this
     Honourable Court, which has jurisdiction to try the suit.
[ ]. That for the purposes of court fee and jurisdiction the suit is valued at Rs. [ ]
     and the requisite court fee of Rs. [ ] is affixed. [Adjust per relief type.]

PRAYER

It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Honourable Court may be pleased to
grant a decree in favour of the plaintiff and against the defendants:
   (a) [primary relief];
   (b) [consequential relief];
   (c) costs of the suit; and
   (d) any other relief this Honourable Court deems just and proper.

                                                    Plaintiff
                                        Through: [Advocate], Advocate

VERIFICATION
Verified at [city] on ___ / ___ / 20__ that the contents of paras __ to __ are true to
the plaintiff's knowledge and paras __ to __ on information and belief, and nothing has
been concealed.

                                                    Plaintiff
```

## Rules

- **Order VII R.1 particulars**: court name; plaintiff's and defendant's names/descriptions/residences; whether plaintiff/defendant is a minor or of unsound mind; the facts constituting the cause of action and when it arose; facts showing jurisdiction; the relief claimed; valuation for court fee and jurisdiction.
- **Cause of action** must be pleaded as a distinct paragraph, with date and place.
- **Jurisdiction and valuation** each get their own paragraph near the end.
- **Relief** in the prayer must correspond to the facts pleaded; do not pray for relief not founded in the body.
- A plaint is typically **accompanied by an affidavit** and a list/schedule of documents relied upon (Order VII R.14) — add a "LIST OF DOCUMENTS" heading if the user supplies documents.
