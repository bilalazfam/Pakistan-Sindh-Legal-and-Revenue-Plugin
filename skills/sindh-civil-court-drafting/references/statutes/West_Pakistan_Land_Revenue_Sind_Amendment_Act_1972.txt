                            SIND ACT NO.IX OF 1972
     THE WEST PAKISTAN LAND REVENUE (SIND AMENDMENT) ACT, 1972
                                                                          [18th July, 1972]

        An Act to amend the West Pakistan Land Revenue Act,
1967, in its application to the Province of Sind.

         WHEREAS it is expedient to amend the West Pakistan Preamble
Land Revenue (Sind Amendment) Act, 1967, in its application to
the Province of Sind.

         It is hereby enacted as follows:-

1.     This Act may be called the West Pakistan Land Short title
Revenue Sind (Amendment) Act, 1972.

2.        In the West Pakistan Land Revenue Act, 1967 (West General
Pakistan Act XVII of 1967), hereinafter referred to as the said amendment
Act, for the word “estate” wherever occurring the word “deh”
shall be substituted.

            to time but no such revision shall take place within
