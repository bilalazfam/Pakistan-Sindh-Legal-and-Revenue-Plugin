                            SINDH ACT NO. III OF 2008
                THE SINDH LAND REVENUE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2008.

                                                                 [1st September, 2008]

           An Act to amend the Sindh Land Revenue Act, 1967.

         WHEREAS it is expedient to amend the Sind Land Revenue Act, Preamble.
     1967, in the manner hereinafter appearing:

     It is hereby enacted as following:-

1    (1)    This Act may be called the Sindh Land Revenue (Amendment) Short title and
            Act, 2008.                                                commence
                                                                      ment.
     (2)    It shall come into force at once.


2.          In the Sind Land Revenue Act, 1967, after section 6, the following Insertion of
     new section shall be inserted :-                                          section 6-A
                                                                               in W.P Act
            "6-A. Renaming of a District or a Taluka.- Notwithstanding the No. XII of
     provisions contained in sections 5 and 6, if the provincial assembly has 1967.
     passed or passes a resolution to the effect that a District or a Taluka
     may be re-named, the Government shall, by a notification, re-name
     such District or Taluka, as the case may be.".
