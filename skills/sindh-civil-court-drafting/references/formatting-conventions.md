# Formatting Conventions (Sindh civil courts)

Shared building blocks used across all document types.

## Cause title (heading)

```
IN THE COURT OF THE [ordinal] [SENIOR CIVIL JUDGE / CIVIL JUDGE / DISTRICT JUDGE],
[CITY], [DISTRICT], SINDH
```
Examples of designation lines the user may need (confirm the exact one from their filed papers):
- `IN THE COURT OF THE VIIth SENIOR CIVIL JUDGE, HYDERABAD`
- `IN THE COURT OF THE IIIrd CIVIL JUDGE & JUDICIAL MAGISTRATE, HYDERABAD`
- `IN THE COURT OF THE DISTRICT JUDGE, HYDERABAD`

Below the court line: the suit number and year, then the parties.

## Suit number line

`[F.C.] Suit No. ______ of 20__`  — leave the number blank for a fresh plaint; fill it for
any document filed in a pending suit.

## Parties block

```
[Name], S/o / D/o / W/o [ ], adult, Muslim/[ ], R/o [address]        ...... Plaintiff

                        VERSUS

1. [Name], S/o [ ], R/o [ ]                                          ...... Defendant No. 1
2. [Name] ...                                                       ...... Defendant No. 2
```
- Use "VERSUS" (or "V/S") between the arrays.
- Number every defendant. Refer to them afterwards as "the defendant No. 1", etc.
- For heirs: "Heirs of [deceased], namely (i) [ ], (ii) [ ] ...... Defendant No. [ ]".

## Nature-of-document line

A single centred line stating what the document is and its purpose, e.g.
`SUIT FOR SPECIFIC PERFORMANCE OF CONTRACT AND PERMANENT INJUNCTION`
`WRITTEN STATEMENT ON BEHALF OF DEFENDANT NO. 6`
`APPLICATION UNDER ORDER 39 RULES 1 & 2 CPC FOR TEMPORARY INJUNCTION`

## Opening

`Respectfully Sheweth:` (plaints, WS, applications) — then numbered paragraphs.

## Prayer

Introduce with: `It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Honourable Court may be pleased to:` followed by lettered reliefs (a), (b), (c) …, always closing with costs and a general/equitable relief clause.

## Verification (pleadings — Order VI Rule 15 CPC)

```
Verified at [city] on this ___ day of ______ 20__ that the contents of paragraphs __ to __
are true to my knowledge and paragraphs __ to __ are believed to be true on information
received / legal advice, and that nothing material has been concealed therefrom.
```
Split the paragraphs between "true to knowledge" and "believed true on information/belief".

## Signature block

```
                                                    [Plaintiff / Defendant No. __ / Applicant]
                                        Through: [Advocate name], Advocate [High Court]
```

## Affidavit (applications, and where a pleading must be supported)

Sworn/affirmed before an Oath Commissioner; deponent competent to the facts. See
`application-and-affidavit.md` for the full skeleton.

## Court fee / valuation

- Declaration, injunction, recovery, specific performance and cancellation suits attract
  court fee tied to the valuation of the relief.
- Add a valuation-and-court-fee paragraph near the end of a plaint. Insert `Rs. [ ]`
  placeholders — never state a fee figure the user hasn't given.

## General

- Formal third-person register; numbered paragraphs; sub-paragraphs (a),(b),(c).
- A4, generous left margin for binding, double or 1.5 line spacing is typical — confirm
  against the user's filed format.
- English is standard in Sindh civil court pleadings; keep statutory terms as-is.
