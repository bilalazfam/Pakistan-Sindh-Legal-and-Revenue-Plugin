# Land revenue and inheritance in Sindh: working note

## Sindh Land Revenue Act text

The Act was enacted in 1967 as the **West Pakistan Land Revenue Act, 1967** (W.P. Act XVII of 1967); in Sindh it
is the **Sindh Land Revenue Act, 1967**. Use the consolidated Sindh text in the `sindh-land-revenue-law` skill. Never
quote a Khyber Pakhtunkhwa or Punjab version for Sindh. Sindh amendments:

| Amendment | File |
|---|---|
| West Pakistan Land Revenue (Sind Amendment) Act, 1972 | `West_Pakistan_Land_Revenue_Sind_Amendment_Act_1972.txt` |
| Sindh Land Revenue (Amendment) Act, 2008 | `Sindh_Land_Revenue_Amendment_Act_2008.txt` |
| Sindh Land Revenue (Amendment) Act, 2013 | `Sindh_Land_Revenue_Amendment_Act_2013.txt` |
| Sindh Land Revenue (Amendment) Act, 2023 (amends s.4) | `Sindh_Land_Revenue_Amendment_Act_2023.txt` |
| **Sindh Land Revenue (Amendment) Act, 2026, Sindh Act XII of 2026, 08-04-2026** (amends ss.4 and 39; inserts ss.42-A and 42-B: computerised land records, biometric verification through NADRA, electronic transfer of title, audit trail) | `Sindh_Land_Revenue_Amendment_Act_2026.txt` |

The **Sindh Land Revenue Rules 1968** are Sindh's own; obtain an official copy before quoting.

## Revenue record vocabulary (Sindh)

- **Deh**: revenue estate (village). **Survey No. / R.S. No.**: revenue survey number.
- **Record of rights**: VF-VII-A (consolidated record, e.g. "Entry No. [x] of VF-VII-A of [year]"); **VF-VII-B**:
  register of changes, each change a **Jiryan** (entry) number.
- **Foti khata**: mutation of inheritance after a death, recording the heirs and their shares.
- **Mukhtiarkar** (taluka revenue officer), **Tapedar** (village record keeper, the Sindh equivalent of patwari),
  **Sub-Registrar** (registers deeds under the Registration Act 1908).
- **DK Book**: older register of family partitions and entries (e.g. "DK Book No. [x], Entry No. [y]").
- **Area**: 1 acre = 40 ghuntas; 1 ghunta = 121 square yards (4,840 sq yd ÷ 40). Written "acres-ghuntas",
  e.g. 23-34 = 23 acres 34 ghuntas.
- **Shares** are expressed as paisa of the whole holding (e.g. "0-1.25 paisa"); read the scale from the
  entry itself before calculating.

## Inheritance shares (Hanafi law): the common cases

These follow the Hanafi fixed shares; confirm with the controlling authority before pleading.

| Heir | No child of the deceased | With a child (son or daughter) |
|---|---|---|
| Widow | 1/4 | 1/8 |
| Husband | 1/2 | 1/4 |
| One daughter, no son | n/a | 1/2 |
| Two or more daughters, no son | n/a | 2/3 shared |
| Son(s) and daughter(s) | n/a | residue, son = 2 × daughter |
| Brothers and sisters (no child, no father) | residue after spouse, brother = 2 × sister | excluded by a son; take residue with daughters only |

Worked example (estate of 5 paisa; widow, brother, two sisters):
- No child: widow 1.25; residue 3.75 split 2:1:1 → brother 1.875, each sister 0.9375.
- With one daughter: widow 0.625; daughter 2.5; residue 1.875 → brother 0.9375, each sister 0.46875.

Grandchildren of a predeceased child: Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961, s.4 (`statutes/Muslim_Family_Laws_Ordinance_1961.txt`).

## Other land statutes bundled

- Specific Relief Act 1877: s.12 (specific performance), s.39 (cancellation of written instruments), s.42
  (declaration of status or right; **substituted by Act XXXIII of 2023**, whose new sub-section (2) requires a
  s.42 suit to be decided within **six months** and the appeal within **ninety days**), s.54–56 (perpetual
  injunction).
- Transfer of Property Act 1882: s.41 (ostensible owner), s.53-A (part performance), s.54 (sale), s.55
  (seller's duties, covenant of title), s.122–129 (gift).
- Registration Act 1908: s.17 (compulsory registration), s.49 (effect of non-registration).
- Stamp Act 1899: impounding of insufficiently stamped instruments (Chapter IV) [check the Sindh schedule].
- Partition Act 1893: s.2, sale instead of division in partition suits.
- Land Acquisition Act 1894. Illegal Dispossession Act 2005. Sindh Civil Courts Ordinance 1962 (civil courts and
  their jurisdiction). Court Fees Act 1870 and Suits Valuation Act 1887 (valuation and fee; Sindh amendments
  to check). Limitation Act 1908. Succession Act 1925 (succession certificates). NADRA Ordinance 2000.

Check every section number above against the bundled text before it goes into a pleading.
