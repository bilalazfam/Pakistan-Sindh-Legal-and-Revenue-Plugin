# Pakistan Legal Toolkit

Three skills for civil litigation in Sindh, Pakistan:

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| pakistan-civil-law-research | Finds the governing statutes and on-point Supreme Court / High Court judgments and applies them to the facts; produces a research memo and a table of authorities. Bundles official texts of 22 laws. |
| sindh-civil-court-drafting | Drafts plaints, written statements, applications, replies, affidavits and legal notices in Sindh civil-court format. |
| sindh-land-revenue-law | Verified Sindh Land Revenue Act text (as amended to 2026), the remedy ladder (appeal, 30-day revision, Board review), and the Revenue Jurisdiction Act 1876 / Board of Revenue Act 1957 provisions. |

Statute texts are working copies; check against the Sindh Code, Pakistan Code or Gazette before filing. Never cite a case that has not been retrieved and read.

## Important — not legal advice

This plugin helps with legal research and drafting. It is **not legal advice** and does not replace a qualified
advocate. Statute texts are working copies taken from official government sources (Pakistan Code, Sindh Code,
Government of Sindh websites); the consolidated Sindh Land Revenue Act is an unofficial consolidation, and provisions
marked **[VERIFY]** have unresolved discrepancies. Always check every provision against the official Gazette or code,
and every case citation against the law reports, before filing anything in court.

## Install

Download this repository as a ZIP and upload it in Claude under **Customize > Plugins**, or install it from the
Claude directory once listed.
